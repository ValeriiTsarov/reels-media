Privacy Policy

Aiwa Reels — internal publishing tool for TikTok
Developer: Aiwa Int · Contact: aiwainit2023@gmail.com
Last updated: 4 November 2026

Aiwa Reels is a private tool that Aiwa Int uses to send short videos about its own games to its own TikTok account as drafts. It has no public sign-up, and nobody else logs in to it.

1. Data used

  • Account access. The tool uses access and refresh tokens for the Aiwa Int TikTok account to upload drafts and check their processing status. These are stored only on the developer's own computer.
  • Published content. The videos are created by Aiwa Int and sent to TikTok.

2. What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal data about people who view, like or comment on the videos.
  • We do not sell or share data obtained through the tool, or use it for advertising.

3. Data deletion

The tool holds no data about other people, so there is nothing of theirs to delete. To ask a question or make a request, email aiwainit2023@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days.

4. TikTok

Videos on TikTok are also subject to TikTok's Privacy Policy.
